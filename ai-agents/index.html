Official Read-Only Rust MCP Server: dark-gravity-mcp

Located in lgcorzo.github.io/dark-gravity-mcp (Rust Edition 2024, rmcp SDK). It is strictly read-only, completely decoupled from the privileged runtime gateway (factory-mcp-server), and exposes six tools plus seven dark-gravity:// resources:

  • Tools: get_architecture, get_research, get_repository_map (includes has_repositories_md), get_adrs, get_experiments, get_metrics.
  • Resources: dark-gravity://manifest, dark-gravity://knowledge-graph, and the five dark-gravity://auditor/* documents.

Compliance wording: No third-party certification (EU AI Act, SOC 2 or other) is claimed. Unmeasured aggregate KPIs remain labeled UNMEASURED (baseline_pending).
