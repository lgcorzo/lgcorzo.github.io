About Dr. Luis Galo Corzo

Bridging Physical Systems with Governed AI

With a Ph.D. in Engineering (Electronics & Machine Vision) and 15+ years in critical physical systems (railway traction converters at CAF, electric transit power at JEMA/Irizar, industrial 3D vision robotics at Inser), I lead the AI department at Lantek Sheet Metal Solutions. My focus is taking artificial intelligence from experimental notebooks into reproducible, observable, testable, and secure industrial production platforms.

Advanced Systems R&D

Autonomous Software Factory — Dark Gravity

An autonomous CA/CD software factory blueprint in Rust. AI agents coordinate Hatchet DAG workflows to plan tasks, synthesize code, and test inside isolated gVisor sandboxes — under strict Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) gates. Demonstrating how autonomous AI can be safely governed within auditable enterprise boundaries.

THE PROBLEM

AI coding agents are powerful but risky: they hold credentials, run untrusted code and act without oversight.

THE APPROACH

Give each agent a narrow role, a disposable sandbox, short-lived credentials and a durable, auditable workflow.

THE OUTCOME

Fast autonomous delivery that regulated teams can trust — with a human gate before every merge.
