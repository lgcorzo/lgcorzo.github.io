Human Authority

The 4-Vertex Zero-Friction Governance Mesh

Dark Gravity rejects unsupervised AI hallucination. The autonomous loop operates under an "On-The-Loop" supervisory model where human engineers maintain sovereign authority at four strategic vertices.

Vertex 1 • Injection

Strategic Goal Injection

The human Product Owner creates high-level Epics or Issues tagged with autonomous-mission. Agents remain blind to work until explicitly authorized by a human operator.

Vertex 2 • Mobilization

Sprint Mobilization Gate

Rustant parses the requirements and synthesizes complete Spec-Kit artifacts (spec.md, plan.md, tasks.md). A human Tech Lead reviews and unlocks sprint execution.

Vertex 3 • Circuit Breaker

Exception Override

If an agent encounters 3 consecutive test failures, diff deadlocks, or spend velocity > +$1.00/60s, the CircuitBreaker trips. The DAG halts, credentials revoke, and a human Architect is summoned.

Vertex 4 • Scrutiny

Categorical Merge Approval

Agents are physically prohibited from auto-merging into main or production branches. A Senior Human Reviewer audits the Pull Request, verifies CI evidence, and manually executes the merge.
